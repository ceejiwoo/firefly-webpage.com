Who We Are

MORE THAN
A GAME.

Firefly is built around the idea that Ultimate Frisbee is more than throwing a disc. It's about trust, communication, movement, and the people beside you.

Whether we're competing on tournament day or training under the afternoon sun, we show up for one another and keep pushing forward.

Mission To build a team that grows through understanding, discipline, teamwork, and continuous learning. We strive to develop players who understand the game, support their teammates, and help one another become better both on and off the field.
Vision To become a united and disciplined Frisbee community where every player understands their role, learns from one another, and inspires others through their actions. We aim to grow not only as athletes, but as people who can lead, encourage, and guide others.
